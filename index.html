KQueue

PHP · Swoole · Feb 2026

A non-blocking runtime for Laravel queues. Every job runs in its own Swoole coroutine, so jobs waiting on the database or an HTTP call no longer hold up the next one. Three jobs that each wait two seconds on I/O finish in about two seconds on one thread, with no changes to existing job code. CPU-heavy jobs are routed to isolated child processes.
